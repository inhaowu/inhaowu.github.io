- Proposed a multi-modality MRI-based framework for automated osteosarcoma segmentation.
- Employed multi-modality feature fusion and recalibration to aggregate complementary information across MRI modalities.
- Integrated joint semantic-boundary learning to address tumor heterogeneity and boundary ambiguity.
