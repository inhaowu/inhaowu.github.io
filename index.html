🔬 Research

My research focuses on medical imaging and multimodal large language models (MLLMs). I develop methods for 3D medical image segmentation and anatomical shape analysis, and investigate how MLLMs integrate imaging evidence with clinical knowledge and guidelines to support clinical reasoning in applications such as automated radiotherapy planning. I also study guideline learning and rule-grounded reasoning in language models, focusing on how to represent interdependent rules and track their applicability during inference.
